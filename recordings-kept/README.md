# Recordings worth keeping

Raw BLIPR captures (see firmware/README.md for the format) kept in git as test cases for `firmware/test/replay`.

- `2026-11-26_22lr_cci_sv/` (s01041-s01045): the first real echoes. .22LR CCI Standard Velocity, Sean's rifle, Nov 26, Teensy 0.2.7 (window 900-1200, quick check off). The echo lasts ~6-8 ms (~2 m), 15-24 dB; the in-band trigger fires 2-4 ms after the muzzle. Teensy 0.2.8 reads 4 of 5: 992, 1029, 1029, 1042 fps. s01046-s01055: two live strings on 0.2.8 (9 of 10 read; s01051 weak). Reference (Athlon Rangecraft, same gun and ammo, Nov 21, 29 shots): avg 1017, SD 15.2, 980-1041.
- `2026-11-27_38spl_athlon/` (s01056-s01068): .38 Special 158 gr handloads (progressive press, charges vary) from a lever-action rifle, BLIPR beside the muzzle, Teensy 0.2.8, window 600-1200, Athlon on the same shots (`athlon.csv`: BLIPR shot number, Athlon fps). s01056 is a 100 ms `rec` with the Athlon on and no shot (no extra lines). Real shots: s01057-s01061, s01063 ("noisy": accel +252), s01064, s01065, s01067, s01068; BLIPR reads 9 of 10 at -3.1 fps avg vs the Athlon (SD of the difference 4.0). s01062 and s01066 are false reads ~0.8 s after a shot (~1135 fps line at +55.7 kHz plus a 250 kHz line: the ESP32 waking to show the shot). s01069-s01078: string 2 on Teensy 0.2.9 (1.5 s hold-off) with lat 0.152 / behind 0.076 (Sean's measurement: 6" beside the bore, 3" behind the muzzle): 10 of 10, no false reads, +3.4 fps vs the Athlon. Replaying both strings, lat 0.13-0.14 / behind 0.076 reads 20 of 20 at -0.2 to +0.2 fps avg (SD of the difference 4.3).
